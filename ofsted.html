The Problem

The inspection starts
before they arrive.

Ofsted rings at half nine and inspectors turn up two working days later. But evidence gathering begins at that phone call — whatever your records look like on the morning of the call is what gets inspected. Two days is not enough time to fix paperwork that has drifted for a year.
