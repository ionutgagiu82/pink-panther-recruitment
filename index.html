Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How quickly can you provide emergency care cover?

Call or WhatsApp us on 07827 358301. We start working on cover straight away and tell you honestly what we can do and how soon. There is no call centre: you speak to a real person who knows the sector.

What checks do your care workers have?

Every worker we place has DBS checks, right to work verification, references and training checked before they are put forward to a service.

Which areas do you cover?

Newcastle upon Tyne, Gateshead, Sunderland, Durham and North Tyneside, plus the wider North East. If you are just outside, ask us.

Which care roles and settings do you supply?

Support workers, senior support workers, residential child care workers, healthcare assistants and carers, and team leaders. We work with supported accommodation, care homes, children's homes, learning disability and mental health services. Nursing and social work staffing are coming soon.

Can you cover long shifts, sleep-ins and waking nights?

Yes. 16-hour shifts, 24-hour rotas, sleep-ins and waking nights are our speciality. They are the shifts other agencies struggle to fill.

How much does it cost?

Rates depend on the role, shift length and how much notice you give. Tell us what you need and we will give you a clear rate before any shift is confirmed.

I am a care worker. How do I get shifts through you?

Register using the form on this page or on our care jobs page, or call us. You are paid weekly.
