Founder & Director

The Person Behind It

"I work in this sector. I know exactly what managers are dealing with when a worker doesn't show up at midnight."

John Gagiu founded Pink Panther Recruitment from direct experience working in supported accommodation and young people's services in Newcastle. Having seen firsthand the chaos that poor agency staffing causes — the last-minute scrambles, the workers who don't understand the service, the managers left without cover — he built an agency designed to actually solve those problems.

Based in Newcastle upon Tyne, Pink Panther Recruitment is focused on the North East, personal relationships, and getting the right people into the right shifts — fast.
