Questions

Care worker questions.

Is the pay weekly?

Yes. Workers placed through Pink Panther Recruitment are paid weekly.

What checks do I need to work through you?

We check DBS, your right to work in the UK, references and your training before we put you forward to a service. Tell us what you already have when you register and we will talk you through anything that is missing.

What kind of shifts are available?

Days, nights, waking nights, sleep-ins and long shifts of 12 to 24 hours, depending on the service. Choose your preferred shifts when you register.

Which areas do you place workers in?

Newcastle upon Tyne, Gateshead, Sunderland, Durham and North Tyneside.

What experience do I need?

Experience in supported accommodation, children's residential care, care homes, or learning disability and mental health support is what services ask for most. Tell us your background and we will say honestly what we have for you.

How do I send my CV?

Email it to john@pink-panther-recruitment.co.uk, then register using the form on this page so we have your phone number and shift preferences.
